Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Lumeo app developed by RaviachStudios.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information directly. However, our app uses Unity Ads to display advertisements. Unity Ads may collect certain information automatically, including your device's advertising identifier and general usage data, in order to serve relevant ads.

Third-Party Services

Our app uses Unity Ads, a third-party advertising service provided by Unity Technologies. Unity may collect and process data in accordance with its own privacy policy, which can be found at https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy.

Advertising

Ads displayed in this app are provided by Unity Ads. The app shows rewarded ads only — they play when you choose to press the "watch an ad" button, and never interrupt you otherwise.

These ads may be personalised based on your interests. If you are in the EEA or the UK, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time from the Privacy row in the app's settings. Refusing means you see non-personalised (contextual) ads instead. You can also opt out of personalised advertising by adjusting your device settings under Google Settings or through your device's privacy controls.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
