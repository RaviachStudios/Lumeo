3Public visibility (leaderboards & Arena)

Lumeo has social features that are, by design, visible to other players. If you sign in, your display name and scores may be shown to other players on global leaderboards and inside multiplayer contests ("the Arena"), including on results screens and podiums. Don't use your real full name as your display name if you'd rather keep it private — a nickname works just as well.

5Advertising & ad mediation

The Application shows ads to keep the game free to play. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay (formerly ironSource), an ad mediation platform operated by Unity Technologies. Mediation means LevelPlay runs an auction across several advertising networks for each ad shown, and the winning network supplies the ad. Your data is therefore shared not only with Unity Technologies but also with the advertising networks participating in that auction.

To select and measure ads, LevelPlay and the networks it mediates may collect and process your device's advertising ID (Google Advertising ID), IP address, device and OS information, and ad-interaction events such as which ads were shown, watched, or clicked. Each of these parties acts as an independent controller of that data. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, which covers Unity LevelPlay.

The networks currently enabled for this Application are listed in the Application's app-ads.txt file, which is the authoritative and up-to-date list of who is authorised to supply and sell ads in the Application.

Two kinds of ad appear: full-screen ads shown when a game ends, and optional "watch a video" ads that you choose to start in exchange for an in-game reward — either to continue playing, or to increase the coins you earned in that game. A one-time in-app purchase is available to remove the full-screen ads; the optional reward videos remain available, because you choose whether to start them.

Your choice about personalized ads

Personalized ads are only shown where you have agreed to them. The Application asks for that consent before any ad is requested, and you can change your answer at any time in the app under Settings → Ad Preferences. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be contextual (non-personalized) and your advertising ID will not be used to profile you.

You can also opt out of personalized advertising at the device level:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

8Delete your account & data

You can delete your Lumeo account (published by Raviach Studios) and the data associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Lumeo.
  2. Tap the settings icon (the gear, top-right of the home screen).
  3. Scroll to the account section and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm on the "Delete Account?" screen.

Your account and its data are deleted straight away, and you are returned to the game as a signed-out guest.

Option 2 — By email (if you've uninstalled the app)

Email the Service Provider using the button below, ideally from the email address associated with your account so the request can be verified:

Request account deletion

Email requests are processed within 30 days.

How to request it

  2. Say which data you want removed — for example your display name, your leaderboard entries, or your contest history.
  3. You'll receive confirmation once it's done. Requests are processed within 30 days.

Request data deletion

  • Leaderboard entries and podium placements

Advertising data is handled separately: to stop your advertising ID being used for personalised ads, open Settings → Ad Preferences in the app and choose generic ads. See Advertising & ad mediation.

10Your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, the right to delete personal information, the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and the right to non-discrimination for exercising these rights. The Service Provider does not sell your personal information for money. However, showing personalized ads involves sharing your advertising ID with Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it mediates for cross-context behavioral advertising, which California and several other US state privacy laws treat as "sharing". You can opt out of that at any time in the app under Settings → Ad Preferences — choosing generic ads there is a complete opt-out of this sharing. To exercise your other CCPA/CPRA rights, contact the Service Provider at raviachstudios@gmail.com.

For a better experience while using the Application, the Service Provider may require certain personally identifiable information, including but not limited to a user ID. This information is retained and used as described in this policy, and may be used to send important notices and, where permitted by law, marketing communications.
